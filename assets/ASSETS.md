# Sindbad portal visual assets

## Logo

The original vector outlines were extracted from the supplied `docs/brand-sheet.pdf`, from the horizontal bilingual logo at the top of the page. These are the original symbol and lettering, not a font recreation.

- `brand/sindbad-logo-white.svg`: white artwork for dark backgrounds, retaining the orange accent.
- `brand/sindbad-logo-color.svg`: teal symbol, charcoal lettering and orange accent for light backgrounds.
- `brand/sindbad-symbol.svg`: original symbol used as the browser icon.

## Editorial photographs

The following five images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this portal. They are illustrative scenes, not photographs of actual Sindbad employees or premises. The original generated files are retained in Codex; optimized WebP copies are included in this project at their original dimensions.

| File | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| `images/team.webp` | Opening hero and company announcements |
| `images/onboarding.webp` | New employee card, training, library |
| `images/design.webp` | Sales and design card, sales banner and documents |
| `images/leadership.webp` | Team manager card, leadership documents |
| `images/workspace.webp` | All employees card, requests, systems, policies and tools |

The existing JPG product photographs remain available for company and brand content.

## Generation prompts

### team

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial website photography for an internal employee portal of a kitchen and home interiors company in Oman. Cohesive premium editorial photo, realistic materials, warm natural daylight, muted teal, pale oak, warm stone and charcoal, subtle film grain, understated and credible. Landscape 16:9 framing. No text, lettering, branding, logos, watermark or collage. Keep important subjects in the middle third vertically so this can crop into a short wide banner. Not a glossy stock-photo pose. Primary request: a welcoming collaborative team in a contemporary kitchen showroom in Oman. Wide architectural photograph, an Omani man wearing a white dishdasha and kumma, a woman wearing a neutral hijab and professional clothing and another designer in smart casual clothing review a material sample at a pale oak kitchen island. People occupy the LEFT half, seen naturally in candid three-quarter profiles at medium distance. Right half is calmer premium dark teal and oak cabinetry with ample negative space for a website headline. Beautiful real kitchen environment, restrained professional atmosphere. No one looking at camera.
